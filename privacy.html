Last updated: August 2026

Going Beyond (operated by Gaurav Adhikari) is the "Data Fiduciary" for the personal data collected through this website, under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What information we collect

When you create an account or place an order, we collect what you provide: your name, email address, phone number, and order details (service, size, deadline, reference files). Optional profile details (address, birthday, design preferences) are collected only if you choose to add them. If you sign up using "Continue with Google," we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from your Google account — nothing more.

Why we collect it, and your consent

We collect this information only to create your account, process and deliver your orders, and contact you about them. By creating an account or placing an order, you consent to this use. You can withdraw consent at any time by requesting account deletion (see "Your rights" below) — though this means we can no longer provide ongoing services like order tracking.

Where your information is stored

Your account and order data is stored securely with Supabase (our database and login provider). This website is hosted on Netlify, and order confirmation emails are sent through Resend. These providers only process your data to help us run the site — they don't use it for their own purposes. We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with advertisers.

Cookies & local storage

When you log in, your session is saved in your browser (using local storage, which works like a cookie) so you don't have to log in again every time you visit. We also remember your dark/light mode choice this way. None of this is used for tracking or advertising. You can clear it anytime by logging out or clearing your browser data.

Your Google account data

If you sign in with Google, our use of your Google account information follows Google's API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements — we use it only to create and manage your account on this site, never for anything else.

Your rights

Under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct or update inaccurate details — most of this you can do yourself from the "My Account" page
  • Withdraw consent and request erasure of your account and data
  • Raise a grievance if you're unhappy with how your data has been handled

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below — we'll respond as soon as possible.

Data retention

We keep your account and order data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to meet legal/tax record-keeping requirements, whichever is longer. You can request deletion at any time as described above.

Contact / Grievance Officer
